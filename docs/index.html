Privacy Policy for TaskEasy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

TaskEasy is an offline-first personal task manager app. Your privacy is fully respected.

1. Information We Collect

TaskEasy does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal or usage data. The app contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics SDKs, and requires no internet permission.

2. Local Data Storage

All tasks, subtasks, categories, timer records, and app preferences you create are stored strictly on your own device in local storage. You can delete all data at any time using Settings → Clear All Data or by uninstalling the app.

3. PDF Export & Sharing

When you choose to export or share a PDF task report, the file is generated locally on your device and saved or shared only to the destination app you explicitly select.

4. Children's Privacy

Because TaskEasy collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
